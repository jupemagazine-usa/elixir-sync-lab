How a banned anthem became a billion-dollar wellness brand.

Social algorithms flag tracks called Drink, Mushrooms, and Weed — titles that name legal botanicals. So we invented a 100% legal botanical elixir, ran clean wellness ads — with the song playing underneath — and let the platforms carry it.

01 — The Opening

Problem. Answer. Result.

The Problem

Algorithms instantly flag or reject ads promoting tracks titled Drink, Mushrooms, and Weed — titles that name legal botanical ingredients.

The Answer

We invented a 100% legal botanical wellness drink. Algorithm-friendly ads for the beverage — with the song playing underneath.

The Result

The wellness ads run clean on the platforms, and engaged listeners find their way to the music.
